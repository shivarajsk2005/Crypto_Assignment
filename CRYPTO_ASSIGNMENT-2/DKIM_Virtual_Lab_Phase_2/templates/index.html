LEARNING OUTPUT

What the Lab Demonstrates

  • SHA-256 creates the email body hash.
  • RSA private key creates the digital signature.
  • The public key verifies the signature.
  • Changing the received message changes its hash.
  • A changed signed message causes verification to fail.
